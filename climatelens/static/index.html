climatelens

Pick anywhere on Earth and see what has already changed there — measured against a 1951–1980 baseline, computed from ERA5 reanalysis rather than recalled from memory.

    Observed: ERA5 reanalysis (ECMWF) via Open-Meteo — a ~25 km gridded record, not the thermometer at your local airport. Two thirty-year climate normals are compared: 1951–1980 (the NASA GISTEMP baseline convention) against 1995–2024.

    Projected: one downscaled CMIP6 model for the 2040s. One model under one scenario — a projection, not a forecast.

    El Niño: the Oceanic Niño Index from NOAA's Climate Prediction Center says which winters since 1950 were El Niño; ERA5 says what those winters were like here. The two are composited locally. Every anomaly is measured against the fitted warming trend, so ENSO is not confused with warming — and it is history, not a forecast for the winter ahead.

    Every number here is computed from that data. The written summary is generated by Claude from those same computed numbers and is not permitted to introduce any of its own.
